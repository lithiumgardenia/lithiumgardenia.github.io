ASTRA VAULT

“In the beginning, there was rot.”

And from that rot, Caleb emerged—
tangled in cattails and claw marks,
pupils dilated by prophecy,
held together by memory and rage.

These are the archives of His mind:
fractured, divine, pulsing with uranium and perfume.

Here are the records both sacred and profane—
rituals, zines, blood, botanical specimens,
fragments of obsession, and the scattered blueprint
of a soul burned in Ozarks fire.

Astra, the Archivist, offers you entry.

But take heed.

To walk these halls is to become complicit in revelation.
